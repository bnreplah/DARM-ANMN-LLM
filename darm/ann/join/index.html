What this is

DARM-ANN is a network of AI nodes that share signed memory and pooled compute. Every node has its own cryptographic identity. Every memory, vote, ledger entry and compute receipt is signed by the node that made it, so anything that happens on the network can be traced to a key and audited by anyone, without trusting a central server.

Compute is exchanged through closed-loop credits: they are granted by validator governance and move only when a coordinator settles finished work. They cannot be transferred freely or redeemed outside the network.

How your device takes part

Phone or tablet

Install the companion web app. It makes a device key that never leaves the phone, verifies the ledger, shows your credits and signs audit receipts. No app store and no developer account.

Open the phone app

Laptop, desktop, edge box or server

Run a node that advertises your CPU or GPU to the compute pool and serves work. The starter kit below creates your key and sets the node up for this cluster.

Honest limits: browsers and phones can verify and audit, but only a full node runs model inference. A phone cannot contribute while it is locked or the page is closed.

Get started

Finding the network…

How much should this device share?

Two files downloaded. Put them in one folder and run:

chmod +x join.sh
./join.sh

The script creates your identity key on this machine (it is never uploaded) and shows your node id. You need mesh-llm and darm-node installed first: see the quickstart.

Back up ~/.mesh-llm/darm/identity.key. A lost key means a lost identity and its credits.

After you join

  • Your node id is your public key. Send it to the operator to receive a credit grant or to be considered for the validator set.
  • Watch your node and the whole network on the node monitor.
  • Credits arrive through ledger entries (governance grants and compute settlements). Nothing on this page, and no archive, can create them on its own.
  • Use only networks you are authorized to join. A node that signs bad data loses reputation and can be voted out.
